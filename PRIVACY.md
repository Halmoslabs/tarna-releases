# Tarna privacy

The standalone download and the Mac App Store edition each have their own notice below.

Tarna Privacy
Updated 27 October 2026

Tarna works on your Mac. We do not collect the content of your screen or keystrokes. The standalone edition checks for updates over the network, so the update host receives ordinary connection information.

Accessibility
With your permission, Tarna uses Accessibility to read the foreground app’s accessible controls, to listen for its shortcut and hint keys, and to focus, click, or scroll the control you choose.

Screen Recording
With your permission, Tarna uses Screen Recording to recognize visible text for keyboard hints.

You can revoke Accessibility and Screen Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

This notice describes the Tarna app from Halmos Labs, Inc. Apple’s services, and support messages you choose to send, are separate.

Contact: https://github.com/Halmoslabs/tarna-releases/blob/main/SUPPORT.md

Tarna Privacy — Mac App Store edition
Updated 27 October 2026

Tarna works on your Mac. We do not collect the content of your screen or keystrokes.

Screen Recording
With your permission, Tarna uses Screen Recording to recognize visible text for keyboard hints.

Input Monitoring
With your permission, Input Monitoring lets Tarna recognize its shortcut and hint keys.

Accessibility
Clicking access appears under Accessibility in System Settings. With your permission, it lets Tarna post the click or scroll you request.

You can revoke Screen Recording, Input Monitoring, and clicking access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

This notice describes the Tarna app from Halmos Labs, Inc. Apple’s services, and support messages you choose to send, are separate.

Contact: https://github.com/Halmoslabs/tarna-releases/blob/main/SUPPORT.md
