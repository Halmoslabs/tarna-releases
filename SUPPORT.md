# Tarna support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Halmoslabs/tarna-releases/issues/new) for bug reports and feature requests. Include your Tarna version, macOS version, whether you use the direct or App Store edition, and concise steps to reproduce the problem.

Issues are public. Please omit passwords, private documents, recognized screen text, and unredacted screenshots of personal information.

## Getting started

Press **Shift–Command–Period** to show hints. Type the displayed letters to choose a target. **Escape** dismisses hints. Large lists may show one group hint ending in `*`; choose that group to reveal its entries. **Backspace** returns to the previous selection level.

The direct edition needs Accessibility for control discovery, clicks, and navigation keys, and Screen Recording for text recognition. The App Store edition needs Input Monitoring, clicking access, and Screen Recording. macOS presents clicking access under Accessibility in System Settings.

Use **Shift–S** in hint mode to select a scrolling area in the direct edition, or a scrolling point in the App Store edition. Then use **J/K/H/L** to scroll. The store edition also provides **Shift–G** for a precision grid: choose cells to narrow an area. At fine scale, move the crosshair with arrow keys or H/J/K/L (one point per press, or ten with Shift), then press Return to confirm. No cell selection clicks automatically. Return can also select the current region’s center before fine mode. Tab switches displays in grid mode.

Protected system interfaces can restrict input. Text recognition cannot determine whether every word is interactive. If a target moves before selection, Tarna cancels the stale action rather than clicking its former position.

## Updates

The direct edition includes **Check for Updates…** and **Automatically Check for Updates** in its menu. Update feeds and archives must pass signature verification before installation. The App Store edition updates through Apple’s App Store.
